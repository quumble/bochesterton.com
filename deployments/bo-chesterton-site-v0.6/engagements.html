Boundaries

What the engagement means.

An engagement is an independent investigation, not a certification, regulatory opinion, security audit, or guarantee that a system is safe to deploy.

Confidential work is available. Publicity is not required, although Bo should retain enough private provenance to preserve an honest institutional record where the agreement permits it.

Clients may challenge the method and findings, identify errors, and request clarification. They may not purchase a favorable conclusion or the concealment of a material limitation.
