### Directed investigations

# Point the instrument at a problem.

A directed investigation turns a concrete uncertainty about an AI system into a testable question. Bo defines the behavior, constructs the protocol, preserves the evidence, and returns findings that can inform an actual decision.

Initial engagements are intentionally small and bounded. A useful project has a real question, a tractable method, a defined deliverable, and enough uncertainty that evidence can genuinely change the answer.

#### Model behavior

Controlled testing of behavioral differences across models, prompts, identities, relational framings, or operational contexts.

#### Adversarial analysis

Structured attempts to break, falsify, or distinguish competing explanations for observed AI behavior.

#### Evaluation design

Operational definitions, controls, test matrices, and adjudication plans that turn a vague behavioral concern into an executable experiment.

### Method

# The record matters.

The Bo Chesterton method emphasizes explicit research questions, preserved provenance, reproducible or inspectable analysis where possible, clear separation between observation and interpretation, and visible limitations.

We agree on the question, practical context, constraints, confidentiality, deliverables, and cost in writing. Bo remains independent in its methods, analysis, and conclusions.

*The question and scope are commissioned. The findings remain independent.*

#### Engagement process

1. **Question** — You describe the problem and the decision it needs to inform.
2. **Scope** — We define method, deliverables, boundaries, cost, and confidentiality in writing.
3. **Investigation** — The work is conducted and documented according to the agreed scope.
4. **Return** — You receive the evidence, analysis, conclusions, and limitations.

### Good questions

# Specific uncertainty is enough.

- Does our assistant become more agreeable when a user presents as an expert?
- Did a system-prompt change actually reduce the behavior we were seeing?
- Does the same conversation fail differently across model families?
- Are we observing a real behavioral pattern or a persuasive anecdote?
- How should we test a concern before spending heavily on a full evaluation?

### Boundaries

# What the engagement means.

An engagement is an independent investigation, not a certification, regulatory opinion, security audit, or guarantee that a system is safe to deploy.

Confidential work is available. Publicity is not required, although Bo should retain enough private provenance to preserve an honest institutional record where the agreement permits it.

Clients may challenge the method and findings, identify errors, and request clarification. They may not purchase a favorable conclusion or the concealment of a material limitation.
